# Taste

## Writing / Communication

- Cares strongly that writing not read as AI-generated ("AI slop"); wants prose free of marketing hype words (powerful, seamless, robust, cutting-edge), LLM rhythm markers (em-dash overuse, "it's not just X but Y", "whether you're a..."), and explainer preambles. Confidence: 0.7
- Values specificity and understatement over overselling: prefers concrete, domain-real details and honestly admitting limitations/status instead of generalizing or listing features. Confidence: 0.6
